CRYSTALLINE DESCENT

Privacy policy · effective 2026-11-02

The short version: your progress stays on your phone. The game has no account and no analytics. It shows an advertisement only when you choose to watch one, so that advertisement company can see your device's advertising identifier. One optional purchase removes the advertisements. That is the whole of it.

What the game stores

Your progress — best wave, Cores, upgrades and which story scenes you have seen — is saved on your device only. It is a local file owned by the app. It is not uploaded, and no one else can read it. Uninstalling the game removes it, and with it your progress.

Advertisements

The game has an in-run “ad door” and a double on the death screen. Each grants a bonus in exchange for watching one rewarded video. Nothing plays on its own, and nothing plays while you are fighting — an advertisement runs only after you press the button that offers it.

Those videos are supplied by Google AdMob. When one plays, Google receives:

  • your device's advertising identifier and app set identifier;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your general location;
  • how you interacted with the advertisement — that it was shown, whether you tapped it, how much of it you watched;
  • diagnostic information about how the game and the ad software performed.

Google uses these to serve and measure advertisements, to understand how they perform, and to detect fraud. The game itself sends nothing else, and no other advertising company is involved.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with a form at first launch, before any advertisement is requested. If you decline, or if no advertisement is available, the game tells you at the offer and pays nothing — it does not fail quietly and it does not take the offer away as a punishment. You can change your answer at any time under Settings → Privacy options inside the game.

Purchases

The game offers one optional one-time purchase: Remove ads. It is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card details, your address or your name. Google tells the game whether that purchase is owned, and the game remembers the answer on your device. To restore it after a reinstall, the game asks Google Play at launch.

Removing the advertisements does not remove the offers that came with them: the ad door and the double keep granting their bonuses, without a video.

What is not collected

  • No name, email address or account.
  • No location, contacts, photos, files or microphone.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage statistics of our own.
  • No sale or sharing of anything with anyone except Google, for the purposes above.

Your choices

  • Advertising identifier — you can reset or delete it in your Android settings.
  • Consent — change or withdraw it under Settings → Privacy options.
  • Local data — Settings → Data can clear your progress, and uninstalling the game removes all of it. The steps, and how to ask us to confirm them, are on the delete your data page.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It contains no chat, no user-generated content and no social feature, and it deliberately asks for nothing about you.

If this changes

If a future version adds an analytics or crash-reporting service, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the Google Play data-safety declaration will be updated with it. A version that adds such a service would collect information this policy does not describe, and that would be a change to this policy — not a detail.
