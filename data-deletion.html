DELETE YOUR DATA

Crystalline Descent · last updated 2026-11-02

The short version: this game stores nothing on a server, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf. What exists is on your phone, and you can remove all of it in a few taps.

What exists

  • Your progress — best wave, Cores, upgrades, which story scenes you have seen — is a file on your device. It is never uploaded.
  • Your advertising identifier — used by Google AdMob to serve a video when you choose to watch one. It is not stored by us.
  • Your purchase record for the optional "Remove ads" product — held by Google Play, not by us.

Delete it yourself, immediately

  1. In the game: Settings → Data → Reset all progress. This erases your progress and upgrades on this device, and asks you first.
  2. Or uninstall the game. That removes every local file it owns, including the save.
  3. Reset the advertising identifier in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads) if you want Google to stop using the current one.

If you want us to confirm or act

Write to facetgamestudios@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data. Tell us the name of the game and, if the request concerns a purchase, the Google account used to buy it — we need that to find the record Google holds for you.

We reply within 30 days, and sooner in practice. Because the game holds no account and no server-side copy of your progress, there is normally nothing to erase beyond confirming that the steps above removed it.

What we cannot delete for you

Records that Google holds — an in-app purchase, or an advertisement served to your device — are governed by Google's privacy policy. You can reach them from your Google account, and the purchase can also be refunded through Google Play within its refund window.
